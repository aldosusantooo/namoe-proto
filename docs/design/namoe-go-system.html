Placeholder generator rules (/img/[slug], stays a route)

  1. Input: tenant name, category slug, size variant (card 600x600, hero 1200x750, thumb 200x200). The slug hash is no longer used for colour.
  2. Background: the category colour, --color-cat-<slug>. Ink: --color-cat-<slug>-ink.
  3. Texture: white dots, 4px radius on a 32px grid at 0.28 opacity (SVG pattern). No other texture on the art.
  4. Glyph: the category glyph at 0.92 opacity, sized 64% of the short side, anchored top-right, bleeding 6% off the right and top edges.
  5. Name: Fredoka 600, 12% of the short side (72 at 600), ink colour, wrapped at 14 characters (20 on hero), bottom-left with a 40 unit inset, max 3 lines then ellipsis. Thumb variant drops the name and centres the glyph.
  6. Booth code is not part of the art. The card overlays it as a cream sticker (radius 8, shadow-sticker-code) so the same image serves every surface.
  7. Cache: public, max-age one day, immutable; the URL carries the name and category so a rename busts it.
  8. Photos win: when Tenant.logoUrl or photos[0] exists the card uses it and the art is not requested.
