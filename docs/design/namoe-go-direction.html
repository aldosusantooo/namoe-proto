Namoe Go, visual direction

One direction, "Poster on cream": the app is built from the same pieces as Theo's Instagram posters. Cream ground, saturated colour blocks with white type, navy headlines in Fredoka, one texture per screen, the five mascots as art only. Every card has a visible edge (a 2px line plus a 3px solid shadow) instead of the grey blur that vanished on cream. Phones are 390px and scroll. Organizer is 1200px. The last phone is the tenant's own page (P1). Tenant placeholders and the passport carry the most work.

Mascots are approximations, Theo's originals replace them. "Namoe Go" is a text lockup in Fredoka 700, not a wordmark; Theo supplies the real logo. Photos in two tenant cards are stand-ins to show the card holds with real images.

BerandaBlue hero block with the lockup, dates, venue, mascots clear of text. Headline talk, four saturated entry tiles, the next three talks. The day-tabbed schedule is gone from home (it lives in Jadwal).

One alternative, and the pick

A. Poster on creamMy pick

Shown above. Flat saturated blocks, cream edges on cards. Reads as Theo's posters turned into an app. Tradeoff: relies on colour for hierarchy, so the ten category colours must stay disciplined.

B. Sticker book

Every card gets a 2.5px navy outline and a hard navy offset shadow, like die-cut stickers on a page. More playful and more "kids". Tradeoff: heavier on screen, and 24 outlined cards in a grid start to buzz; it also fights the flat posters, which have no outlines at all.

What this direction answers from the audit

  • Placeholder uses the category colour and glyph, so the card agrees with itself and with the map.
  • Favicon and app icon become the blue mascot (spec, no mockup needed).
  • Tab bar icons redrawn at 2.6px stroke, filled active on a yellow pill.
  • All eyebrows in sentence case, Fredoka navy, no uppercase anywhere.
  • Passport has three designed states, a stamp shape, a "baru" moment and a ticket.
  • Peta: legend removed, zoom controls, 44px booths when zoomed, inline sheet with thumb.
  • Beranda no longer duplicates Jadwal; hero and tiles are saturated blocks.
  • Directory header is one row plus chips; badges no longer wrap.
  • Back buttons on tenant page and session board.
  • Sesi: questions first, compose pinned, upvote as a pill, timestamps dropped.
  • Tanya tenant shows one empty state with a mascot.
  • Cards have a visible edge; colour blocks replace pastel wash.
